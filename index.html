The Realities of the Trip

Regardless of your budget, every traveler in Egypt must be prepared to navigate the following baseline realities:

💧 Water Safety

Tap water is not potable. You must strictly use bottled water for drinking and brushing teeth, and avoid raw salads or drinks with ice to prevent stomach illness.

☀️ Extreme Heat & Pacing

Midday temperatures regularly exceed 100°F (38°C). To survive the heat, you must be comfortable waking up at 5:00 AM or 6:00 AM for temple tours.

🗣️ Relentless Hustle Culture

Book a reputable local tour guide in advance to insulate yourself from the pressure from vendors, unsolicited "guides", scams, and harassment.

🚦 Traffic & Chaos

Cairo is notoriously loud, polluted, and chaotic. Crossing the street requires walking directly into moving traffic. It is an overwhelming, but vibrant, sensory experience.
